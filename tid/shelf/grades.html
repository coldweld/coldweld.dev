The grades

A grade says how strong the source behind one number is. Five grades run from a figure a primary source states to a figure the designer chose.

The letter is always printed. A grade does not say the number is right for the game. It says what stands behind it.

Grade A: Primary

A primary source states the figure, and the game uses it as stated.

Example: Hours in one working shift in Hull wear and repair is 8 hours.

2 numbers hold this grade.

Grade B: Secondary

A secondary source quotes the figure, such as Atomic Rockets quoting NASA.

Example: Density of air in Fire and damage control is 1.225 kg per m^3.

40 numbers hold this grade.

Grade C: Derived

The figure is arithmetic from an A or B figure, and the arithmetic is shown.

Example: Energy to warm one kg of tank water to boiling and then boil it off in Heat and radiators is 2,594,400 J per kg.

12 numbers hold this grade.

Grade D: Analogy

A real figure scaled into the setting, with the real figure and the scaling stated.

Example: Fire classes that need live power to start in Fire and damage control is C, K classes.

35 numbers hold this grade.

Grade E: Authored

The game chose the figure for play, and the page says where the sources are silent.

Example: Length of one fight tick, which every fire and vent number below is per in Fire and damage control is 20 seconds.

703 numbers hold this grade.
