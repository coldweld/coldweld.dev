In brief

A contact is another ship or a signal that meets the captain on a leg of a trip. The chance of one depends on where the ship is, what it carries and who is aboard.

Why: the real physics or procedure

  • At cruise the ship moves at hundreds of km/s, so another ship cannot come alongside, board or flag it down.
  • Only a signal from afar or a fault from inside can reach a ship at cruise.
  • A derelict, a debris field or a trade vessel can meet the ship at departure, at arrival or locally.
  • A customs patrol waits at the far end of the leg.
  • A hunting ground is a spot where the captain waits because traffic has to pass, so it changes both how many contacts and who they are.

Where the game departs from reality

  • The day's contact roll clamps at 50, so a bonus alone cannot lift a fight above its cap. A hunting ground therefore raises the hostile share as well as the count.

Where the sources are silent

  • No shelf page or mirror page read for this page gives a rate of contact between planets, so every chance here is authored.
  • No source gives how many bounty hunters hunt a wanted captain.
