In brief

The deck fight is the boarding action inside a ship, fought compartment by compartment through hatches, with the boarders trying to take the bridge, the engine room or the hold.

What the game does

Boarding

  • A raider boards by matching your speed inside 5 km for 20 s.
  • The helm breaks a boarding match unless that procedure asks you first.
  • THE DECK FIGHT | your armed crew and boarding party meet the boarders.
  • You command it, even when the Second flies the ship.
  • GENERAL QUARTERS has the crew suited before they arrive.
  • Win, and that raider has nobody left to board with.
  • Lose, and the raiders fill their hold from yours, the most valuable goods first.
  • They take the ship only if you fought on after the Second's warning.

Why: the real physics or procedure

  • A boarding party must enter by an airlock or cut through the hull, and a defender can wait at the airlock with weapons aimed.
  • Men in a compartment that is vented lose air, so suited men are stunned and unsuited men die.
  • A hatch cut by a team takes time, and the men who come through it fight badly for a moment.
  • A boarding party that loses a share of its men falls back to its pod.

Where the game departs from reality

  • The deck fight plays on a plan of a medium hull drawn on its side. A real hull has far more compartments.
  • The airlocks sit amidships, three cut hatches from the bridge, the engine room and the hold, because an airlock beside the bridge let eight boarders take the bridge in 65 of 66 fights.

Where the sources are silent

  • The mirror gives no time to cut a hatch, to get into suits, or to hold a compartment, and no hit chance for a shot between compartments.
