In brief

A vital is a system a ship cannot fight without, such as the reactor, the cooling fins, the air plant, the drive and the guns, and a ship loses a fight when one of them fails and not when its hull is used up.

What the game does

Life Support

  • Neither side aims at LIFE SUPPORT. A round that gets through the plate gets inside instead.
  • There it can wreck cargo, kill a crewmate, hole the reactor plant or vent a compartment.
  • Otherwise it takes 8 off life support.
  • At 40 or less life support fails.
  • Every 20 s it stays down, bad air can kill a hand at 15%, unless GENERAL QUARTERS has the crew suited.
  • While it is down or its air plant is cut, your guns lay wider and every hand loses morale every 20 s.
  • Life support is mended before every fight.

Why: the real physics or procedure

  • A ship is a set of systems, and a hit on the hull is only dangerous when it reaches one of them.
  • A warship is lost in a fight when a critical system fails and starts a cascade, even if the hull is mostly whole.
  • That is called a mission kill: the ship cannot fight on, though it may still float.
  • The reactor, the cooling fins, the air plant, the drive and the guns are the vitals of a ship in this game.
  • The fins and the air can fail and the ship fights on with penalties, while a reactor with no containment ends the fight.
  • A crew that is tired, sick or caught off its stations shoots worse, and each of those costs aim.

Where the game departs from reality

  • Each vital is a single track from whole to gone. A real ship has many parts of each system, and only the fins are split into panels (docs/notes/hazards.md).
  • The reactor has no floor above a kill: containment at zero ends the fight, while the fins and the air can reach zero and the ship fights on (docs/notes/hazards.md, the reactor).

Where the sources are silent

  • No source gives how much aim a tired, sick or distracted gun crew loses. Every accuracy penalty on this page is the game's own, set against the others in size.
  • No source gives a crippled line for a ship system. The 40 percent line is the game's one shared choice.
