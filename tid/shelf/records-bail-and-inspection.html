In brief

A record is the charges a flag holds against a captain: it fades with time, a captain can be bonded against a hearing, and a port or patrol can search the hold.

Why: the real physics or procedure

  • A State may inspect travellers' health papers and conveyances on arrival or departure (WHO regulations, Article 23.1). That is the nearest shelf rule for a port that searches a ship.
  • Bail is a surety and not a fine: the captain posts it against turning up, and the bondsman's cut is not refundable.
  • A record forgets slowly, so that waiting is a worse deal than paying.

Where the game departs from reality

  • The record forgets one point in 10 days, ten times slower than heat's one point a day (comment in locations/crimes.py).
  • Facing an inspection and bribing past it are game choices with no shelf source.

Where the sources are silent

  • The WHO page covers health inspection and not a search for contraband or the arrest of a wanted captain.
  • A search of the shelf pages for bail, limitation and surety finds a hit only on the Navy handbook page, which was not read for this note.
