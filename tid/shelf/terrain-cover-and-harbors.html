Terrain, cover and harbors

Terrain is the rock a fight happens around: asteroids and dense clouds that a ship can hide behind, crash into, or steer round, and a station harbor that fires on anyone who shoots near it.

Real-world basis

4 points
  • Space is nearly empty, so a body can hide a ship only if it is larger than the ship by a wide margin.
  • A ship that touches rock at speed is destroyed, so the helm keeps a clearance and looks ahead along its path.
  • A line of sight through a dense cloud is blocked once the light is mostly absorbed, which is an optical depth near one.
  • A station garrison fires on a shooter that ignores a warning.

Game and reality differences

2 points
  • Real bodies over 1 km are about 900,000 km apart. The game puts several in one field and a dense cloud inside 300 km, because a fight needs something to hide behind.
  • A cloud is a smooth density and not ten thousand boulders. A real field would be a swarm.

Source absences

5 points
  1. Atomic Rockets does not provide values for the following:
    1. Safe distance from a rock.
    2. Impact speed that destroys a hull.
    3. Size of a station harbor. 2,000 m is authored.
    4. Size of a harbor garrison. 100 marines in a full level 1 garrison is authored.
  2. No source on the shelf provides values for the following:
    1. Bodies in each belt. The orbits are recalled, unverified.
