Production plant condition

A production plant is the mills of a station. Its condition is how close it runs to its specification, which falls when the yard lacks spare parts and when a raid or a siege damages it.

Real-world basis

4 points
  • A yard that holds enough of its spare parts keeps its production plant at specification.
  • Each point of cover under specification costs 1.5 points of condition, down to a floor.
  • A plant closes a fixed share of the gap to its target each day, and a paying captain closes it faster.
  • A raid damages a plant in proportion to the loot, and a siege is assessed when it ends.

Game and reality differences

1 point
  • The news names a fall past two bands (0.9 and 0.8) once, and the return past 0.95 once. Those thresholds are news timing and are excluded from the shelf.
