In brief

A hull class is the size band of a hull, light, medium or heavy craft, and it sets the reactor, the heat capacity, the reactor armor slot and the Safeguards rules for the hull.

Why: the real physics or procedure

  • A hull's points say how much shooting it survives, and its class says what it is.
  • The class sets reactor output, capacitor size, heat capacity, thermal signature, to-hit and the Safeguards license.
  • Class is authored per model, because a save stores the model.
  • Every hull carries one reactor armor slot, sized by its class.
  • A sloped plate reads thicker by the secant of its angle, so a hull sloped on a face stops more there.

Where the game departs from reality

  • The thresholds of 120 and 250 hull points are the fallback only, for a hull nobody authored.
  • The locker is a Ship object with no fuel capacity, so every body reads out of range.

Where the sources are silent

  • The shelf and mirror pages read for this page hold no size bands for craft, so the three classes and every threshold are authored.
