In brief

The power model is the set of rules for the ship's electricity: a reactor plant that fills a battery bank, and a table of loads that draw from it.

What the game does

Power And The Capacitor

  • Power is the electrical cost of fighting, as heat is the thermal one.
  • The CAPACITOR is a bank that starts the fight full.
  • It holds 2.8 times what the reactor makes in 20 s, and the reactor refills it every second.
  • Rail and coil guns draw on it, and so do the pumps.
  • Heavy fire leaves less power for the pumps, and a gun waits on an empty bank.
  • Autocannons and canisters draw nothing.
  • A holed reactor makes less power.
  • SCRAM | with the fins crippled and the tank boiling, the engineer asks to shut the reactor down.
  • Scrammed, it makes decay heat only, and nothing refills the bank.

Why: the real physics or procedure

  • A nuclear plant makes heat, and a generator turns only part of it into electricity.
  • The rest is waste heat, which is why a plant also needs radiators.
  • A reactor makes steady power, but a weapon wants a huge burst, so a ship stores energy in a bank and draws on it for the burst.
  • When a reactor is shut down it still makes heat for a time from the decay of its fission products, so it still needs cooling.
  • A merchant ship has more than one generator, and if power runs short the ship drops its non-essential loads in a set order.
  • That drop must not cut the loads a ship needs to move and stay safe, such as pumps, steering and alarms.
  • The ship's owner may choose the order of the non-essential loads, and on a warship the engineer officer controls the circuits in an emergency.

Where the game departs from reality

  • The sizes in WEAPON_POWER_BY_SIZE are authored so reactor output covers about two Standard guns a round (combat/loaddata.py comment, docs/notes/power.md).
  • The reactor plant is sized from each hull's weapon slots plus pumps for a full drive's heat for one round, not from a real plant design (docs/notes/power.md).
  • The bank starts every fight full, refills each second by the plant's output, and is never carried between fights. The reason is the fight reset in combat/newtonian/world.py (docs/power-direction.md, 'The finding that shapes the arc').
  • What is built and what is not changes stage by stage: docs/power-direction.md, 'Stages', is the current list.

Where the sources are silent

  • No source gives a restart time for a scrammed reactor of this type. The repo marks the restart time as authored and unbuilt (docs/power-direction.md, 'Questions' and 'Austen's answers').
  • The decay heat share of 0.035 is recalled from the Way-Wigner formula and marked unverified in combat/plant.py. The mirror gives no figure for it.
  • No source gives a reefer container's draw or a hotel load per hand beyond the mirror's life support figure of about 30 kW for six crew (rocket/realdesigns3.php.html, cited by docs/power-direction.md, UNVERIFIED here).
  • The mirror gives no figure for a pump's share of the heat it moves. The 1% in PUMP_POWER_SHARE comes from NRC water-reactor pump ratings and is marked unverified for a space plant (docs/notes/plant.md).
  • No source gives a vital-load list for a warship. docs/power-direction.md names NSTM chapter 320 as the likely source, and it was not opened (UNVERIFIED).
  • The Navy damage control manual says only that each repair party restores casualty power, and nothing on load shedding (docs/power-direction.md, 'The sources').
  • The draw of a cartridge gun's feed motor is recalled as about 115 kW for a GAU-8, UNVERIFIED (docs/notes/plant.md).

Sources

  • Atomic Rockets, power, Fission power (SAFE-400 heatpipe reactor): The page lists the SAFE-400 as a 400 kW thermal, 100 kW electric heatpipe reactor, which is a 25% conversion. The game uses that ratio. (all uses)
  • Atomic Rockets, power, batteries table (Lithium-Ion rechargeables): Lithium-ion cells hold about 100 Wh per kg, or 0.36 MJ per kg, with a life of about 400 recharge cycles. combat/power.py cites it for the bank's mass. (all uses)
  • Atomic Rockets, spacewarship, Space Warships: Power Generation, Waste Heat, and Firepower: A warship's power storage can be ultracapacitors, and the page gives 60 Wh per kg and 0.1 TW of discharge for a 5% share of a very large hull. (all uses)
  • Atomic Rockets, spacewarshipart, multi-mode reactor for burst power: A reactor in closed-cycle mode runs at only 3 to 4% of burst power, and its decay heat goes to radiators. Open-cycle cooling is used for bursts. (all uses)
  • Atomic Rockets, spacegunconvent, railgun efficiency: The Navy railgun is no better than 42% efficient, so most of a rail shot's energy becomes waste heat. The repo derives the coil share of 0.2 from it and a large coil's 90 to 95%. (all uses)
  • Atomic Rockets, atomicfuel, Power reactors (decay heat): Short-lived fission products keep heating the fuel after shutdown, and this decay heat sets the cooling needed after a reactor stops. (all uses)
  • UK MCA, MSIS003 Part 7: Electrical Equipment and Installations, 7.6, Load shedding: Shedding must not cut the services essential for propulsion and safety, and the order for non-essential services may suit the owner's convenience. The page is a UK merchant ship surveyor guide, a secondary source here. (all uses)
  • Standard Organization and Regulations of the U.S. Navy (SORM), 6.4.1 e, engineer officer, item 7: The engineer officer controls electrical circuit usage to prevent further damage. SORM writes no order from the commanding officer for it. (all uses)
