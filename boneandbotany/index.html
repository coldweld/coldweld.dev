Bone & Botany

by coldweld

A lab researcher digs real fossils, clones the animals into a park, and the clones meet in simulated hunts.

Artificial Intelligence content disclosure

I use an LLM to write and modify the game's TypeScript code. I design the game systems and mechanics. The animal pictures are silhouettes drawn by human artists, sourced from PhyloPic and credited in the game. An LLM drafted the first version of the game's text and I am rewriting it by hand.

Design Aspects

Sourced numbers:
Every animal number carries its source, or is marked as a game choice.
Odds before the press:
The player sees the cost and the odds of an action before choosing it. The Pick shows its chance to crack bone.
